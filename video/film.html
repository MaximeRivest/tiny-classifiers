10 · Ten tasks
Same recipe, ten tasks
Ettin-17M, one recipe for all, mean of 3 runs, up to 150 examples per category. Test sets: 500 to 3,076 messages (±1.5–2 points). Hate speech: insults toward women are “hate” 68% of the time in training, 42% in the test set.
11 · When it pays
When does fine-tuning pay for itself?
banking77 shown: Kimi K3 labels $14.34, training 44 s on a rented RTX 3090 ($0.12/h); Jev at its measured $43 per million. Across the ten tasks: break-even after 55k–504k messages in money, 44k–534k in time.
